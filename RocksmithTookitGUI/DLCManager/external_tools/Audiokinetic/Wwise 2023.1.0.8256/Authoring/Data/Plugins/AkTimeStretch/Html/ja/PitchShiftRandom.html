Pitch Shift Random

Offsets the Pitch Shift amount specified by a random amount up to plus or minus the Pitch Shift random value. This will be effective for the whole duration of the voice on which the Pitch Shift will be applied. 再生中に受信されるRTPC は、次の再生時に考慮される。

単位: cents

Default value: 0
Range: 0 to 4800
