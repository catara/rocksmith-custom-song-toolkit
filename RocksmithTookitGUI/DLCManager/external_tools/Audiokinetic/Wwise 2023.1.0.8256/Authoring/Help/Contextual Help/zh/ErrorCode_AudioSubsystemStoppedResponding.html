Hardware audio subsystem stopped responding. Silent mode is enabled.

“硬件音频系统停止响应。已启用静默模式”。此错误表示音频驱动程序长时间未对 Wwise 做出响应。This usually mean the audio driver crashed, reconfigured, or reset on its own without notifying the sound engine or the application. 引擎处于安全模式，未选择任何音频设备。音频事件仍将被处理，因此与游戏的同步并未中断。

通常，在系统的音频驱动程序重启后，此错误会自行修复。

On some systems, such as iOS and Android phones, this error could occur upon receiving a system interruption for a phone call, for example. 在中断结束后，音频一般会恢复。
