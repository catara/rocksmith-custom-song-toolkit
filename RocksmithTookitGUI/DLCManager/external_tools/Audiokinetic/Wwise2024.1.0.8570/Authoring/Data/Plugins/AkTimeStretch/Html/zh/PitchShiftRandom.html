Pitch Shift Random

Offsets the Pitch Shift amount specified by a random amount up to plus or minus the Pitch Shift random value. This will be effective for the whole duration of the voice on which the Pitch Shift will be applied. 播放过程中接收到的 RTPC 仅会在下一次播放时考虑进来。

单位：音分

Default value: 0
Range: 0 to 4800
