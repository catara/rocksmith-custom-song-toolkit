Hold Emitter Position and Orientation

若启用，则将存储声音开始播放时游戏对象的瞬时位置和朝向，以及声音播放期间相对该基准位置的偏移。

备注:Wwise Spatial Audio features are disabled for sounds that enable Hold Emitter Position and Orientation. This includes early reflection processing, diffraction and transmission, and room sends.

Default value: false
